Task Specification with Hash-Linked Provenance for LLM Agents, and What Its
Structured Evidence Revealed", submitted to *Automated Software Engineering*)

it. Paths are study-root-relative (`studies/2026q2/` in the repository).

"SI" = the manuscript's Supplementary Information; "§" = manuscript section.
